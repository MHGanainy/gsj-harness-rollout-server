The caretaker of the Altona depot testified that no one entered the customs office between the two May visits. Elbe Warehousing KG disputed the tally of copper fittings, while the Greif Cooperative considered the matter settled since April. Falke Insurance SE retained Hanse Shipping GmbH to appraise the copper fittings, an assignment expected to take 25 weeks. The registry shows that Falke Insurance SE forwarded the warehouse-lease dossier to Elbe Warehousing KG after 8 reminders.

FACT-case_0003-0007-1: The warehouse-lease docket cites permit SN-0300071 issued to the Greif Cooperative.

The records room at the Bremerhaven terminal was opened on a Thursday in the presence of Ottilie Morgenstern and Nikolaus Hofstetter. Ottilie Morgenstern circulated a memorandum on the warehouse-lease file, and the Greif Cooperative acknowledged receipt within 19 days. During the August inspection at the Charlottenburg annex, Nikolaus Hofstetter recorded that the copper fittings had been moved to the customs office. During the January inspection at the Charlottenburg annex, Paul Achterberg recorded that the ceramic insulators had been moved to the clerk's antechamber. Witnesses at the Altona depot recalled that Clara Eichinger and Ottilie Morgenstern disagreed about the warehouse-lease invoice on a Wednesday.

The loading bay at the Charlottenburg annex was opened on a Thursday in the presence of Elena Falkenrath and Clara Eichinger. Elena Falkenrath estimated the ceramic insulators at 1750 marks, a figure the Greif Cooperative called conservative. A clerk noted in the margin that the ceramic insulators were counted twice, once by Elena Falkenrath and once by a representative of the Greif Cooperative. Freight papers countersigned by Clara Eichinger list 16 crates delivered to the Bremerhaven terminal without remark.
