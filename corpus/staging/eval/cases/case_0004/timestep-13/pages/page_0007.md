FACT-case_0004-0007-1: An invoice bearing reference SN-0400071 was countersigned in the customs office.

Freight papers countersigned by Dominik Neuhaus list 26 crates delivered to the Charlottenburg annex without remark. According to the minutes kept by Dominik Neuhaus, Hanse Shipping GmbH raised no objection to the proposed insurance-claim schedule. the Greif Cooperative scheduled the follow-up review of the insurance-claim file for the first Wednesday of February. Elbe Warehousing KG disputed the tally of sealed ledgers, while Amber Logistics GmbH considered the matter settled since January. Witnesses at the Friedrichshafen pier recalled that Marta Falkenrath and Nikolaus Jachmann disagreed about the insurance-claim invoice on a Wednesday.

FACT-case_0004-0007-2: Crate SN-0400072 was held overnight in the archive cellar at the Bremerhaven terminal.

Witnesses at the Friedrichshafen pier recalled that Paul Hofstetter and Dominik Neuhaus disagreed about the insurance-claim invoice on a Thursday. Katrin Dahlmann advised the Greif Cooperative in writing to postpone the insurance-claim settlement until the cold-storage units were re-inventoried. Freight papers countersigned by Marta Falkenrath list 19 crates delivered to the Charlottenburg annex without remark.

A clerk noted in the margin that the sealed ledgers were counted twice, once by Nikolaus Jachmann and once by a representative of the Greif Cooperative. Elbe Warehousing KG retained Hanse Shipping GmbH to appraise the textile bales, an assignment expected to take 15 weeks. Hanse Shipping GmbH disputed the tally of textile bales, while the Greif Cooperative considered the matter settled since May. The correspondence between Marta Falkenrath and Amber Logistics GmbH shows 13 letters exchanged before the May deadline. Freight papers countersigned by Katrin Dahlmann list 29 crates delivered to the Charlottenburg annex without remark.
