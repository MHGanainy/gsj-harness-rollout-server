repository's **shape**, not its content.

prompts, 50 reports, 26 ADRs, an audit, eight evidence directories, 152

## Step 1 — Who reads this repo, and what each needs

- **the consumer** — `pip install`s the library, may never clone at all.
  What do they need *in the repo*? Probably: README, the corpus
  contract, a licence, and pointers.
- **the operatate up. Needs `staging/`,
  `forgejo/`, `mcp-service/`, `corpus/`, `pins/`.
- **the contributor** — changes the library. Needs the code, the tests,

- **the auditor** — checks a claim. Needs everything, and specifically

## Step 3 — The options, costed
At least these three, and any better one you find. **Do not pre-commit
to my preference** — cost them and recommend.

**(a) Reorganize in place.** The record moves under one directory
(`record/` or similar) with an index; the top level shows code, tests,
consumer docs, and the estate. `.gitattributes` marks the archive
`linguist-documentation` so the language bar tells the truth.
*Cost*: every citation of `docs/reports/CP-NN.md` in three repos and
forty-five reports breaks. Count them. Is a path-stable move possible
(a symlink, a redirect index, or leaving `docs/` and adding a curated
top level instead)?

**(b) Split the record into its own repo.** Library keeps code, tests,
consumer docs; `-record` takes prompts, reports, ADRs, goldens,
evidence. *Cost*: cross-repo citations, two clones for an auditor, and
the audit's own "the record is the strongest asset" argument now sits
behind a URL. Also: which repo do the ADRs belong to? They are deons
about the code.

**(c) Leave the tree, fix the framing.** A top-level `docs/README.md`
index, a `.gitattributes`, and a README section that tells a reader what
to ignore. *Cost*: nearly nothing, and it may be insufficient — a
listing of 300 files is not fixed by a paragraph.

Also cost, separately: **`corpus/staging/` — 152 pages of synthetic
legal documents.** It is the pipeline's test data, ships in nothing, and
is the largest directory by file count. Options: keep, generate at test
time, or move to a fixture archive. What breaks in each case — the
lock's SHAs, the golden's provenance, `verify`'s determinism?
