## Step 4 — Sync and prove it took
The half that distinguishes a loop from a batch job.

Push the updated weights by the Step-1 mechanism, then **prove the next
collection ran against them**. Options, pick one and say why: the serving
engine reports the new adapter or checkpoint; a probe request's output
changes deterministically; the policy version increments and rides a
subsequent trace.

If the sync cannot be proven — only asserted An unproven sync is
the difference between "we ran a loop" and "we ran two batches."
