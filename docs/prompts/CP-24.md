## Step 1 — ADR: what a row is, and what it is for
ADR-0003 deferred on the grounds that Polar takes tasks, not parquet
rows. That reasoning still holds — so be explicit about what the bank is
*for* here, because it is not what it was for in the predecessor:

- **Not** an input Polar consumes. Polar takes a `TaskRequest`.
- **Is** the enumeration a consumer needs: which `(case, timestep,
  prompt)` triples exist, which split each belongs to, and what a
  submitter passes to `render_task_request`. Today a consumer enumerates
  by hand — that is Phase D's complaint.

Decide and record:
- **The row shape.** The predecessor's §3.1 was `(data_source, prompt,
  extra_info, split)`. Ours needs at minimum the triple, the split, and
  the prompt — plus whatever `render_task_request` takes
  (`prompt_source`, `skill_card_text` when a skill resolves). Say which
  fields, why each, and what a consumer does with the row.
- **Skill resolution.** A skill row's card lives in `skills/<name>/`.
  Does the bank carry the resolved text, the card's hash, a reference, or
  nothing? **This decides G1's story**: CP-13 landed a gate that verifies
  a *stated* hash; the bank is where the statement can come from
  honestly. stronger instrument hashes from the
  episode's own checkout, and this CP does not build that.
- **Free rows** carry verbatim text (the contract's shape). Confirm.
- **Determinism.** The bank must reproduce byte-identically from an
  unchanged tree, like every other artifact here. Say how (sorted order,
  no timestamps, canonical types).
