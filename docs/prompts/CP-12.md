## Step 3 — The verdict
Structure it as five questions, each answered with evidence and citations
to the reports.

**1. What did Polar give us?** Be concrete and quantified: the episode
lifecycle, the proxy capture across provider dialects, trajectory
reconstruction, async staging, two runtimes, nine harness presets. Against
the predecessor's ~1,800 lines of the same layer. And the parts nobody
would have built: prewarming, Apptainer, the rollout API.

**2. What did it cost?** The three carried patches and their re-vendor
burden (CP-03 measured half a day). The nine silent-degradation modes
(CP-05's S1–S9), three of which you hit live. The validation you had to
write *because* the layer degrades silently rather than failing. The
release-less vendoring posture. Be honest that some of this is the price
of harness-agnosticism rather than a defect.

**3. What do we still own, and is it the right set?** The corpus pipeline,
the MCP service with token-scoped cutoffs, the harness, the builder
subclass, the checks. Ask the sharp version: *is any of it something Polar
should be doing?* And the sharper one: is any of it something a trainer
should be doing instead?

**4. Does it work?** The success criterion is met on the Mac pair —
say exactly what that means and what it does not. Masks exact,
sampling-independent tokens byte-identical, no divergence attributable to
Polar. **And the honest gap: nothing has been trained.** The predecessor
trained three regimes end to end with recorded runs; this has produced
traces and proved they match. Both are real; they are not the same
achievement, and the verdict must say so plainly.

**5. Adopt or not?** One of:
- **ADOPT** — with the conditions that must hold (the H200 pair, M4's
  training loop) and what would reverse it.
- **ADOPT PROVISIONALLY** — the honest answer if the evidence supports the
  architecture but not yet the outcome. Say what converts it to a full
  adopt.
- **DO NOT ADOPT** — name the §9 condition and cost Path C.

Do not inflate and do not hedge into meaninglessness. A provisional adopt
with named conditions is a real answer; "it depends" is not.
