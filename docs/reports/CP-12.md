#### The verdict, in brief (the five answers — full text in `docs/VERDICT.md`)

1. **What did Polar give us?** The whole ~1,800-line predecessor layer —
   episode lifecycle, proxy capture across dialects, trajectory
   reconstruction, async staging — as ~14,200 lines of theirs driven by
   1,496 of ours (only ~400 of which touch execution), plus what nobody
   here would have built: eleven harness presets, the `import_path` and
   builder-registry seams, Apptainer, evaluator prewarming, the rollout
   API. Reconstruction was line-verified (CP-05) and then matched the
   golden empirically (CP-09: masks exact, `prompt_ids` byte-identical,
   capture agreement mean |Δ| = 0.000114).
2. **What did it cost?** Three hand-adapted carried patches on a
   release-less pin (~1 day first vendor, ~half-day re-vendor estimate,
   the pending `prefix_merging` refactor the named risk); a layer whose
   universal failure mode is silent degradation to COMPLETED (S1–S9
   catalogued; S1, S4′, and the off-catalogue F1 hit live), which is why
   566 lines of builder+checks and the pins walk had to exist. Some of
   that bill is the price of harness-agnosticism, not defect — and every
   cure landed in our code, never in a fork.
3. **What do we still own, and is it the right set?** The corpus, the
   MCP service with verified-claim cutoffs, the harness contract, the
   validating builder, the checks — nothing that stores, schedules,
   scores, weights, versions, or trains. The honest concessions: the
   glue stitch and the S-checks are arguably Polar's job done by us at
   the pin (the stitch is already scheduled to go dormant at the
   template flip), and `client.py` is the trainer's leg living here as
   the reference implementation of law 6.
4. **Does it work?** The §5 success criterion is MET on the Mac pair —
   masks exact, sampling-independent tokens byte-identical, no
   divergence attributable to Polar (CP-09, PASS WITH FINDINGS) — with
   its limits stated: Mac numerics do not govern (A-16), the pins were
   validated on the same two episodes they were derived from, and **the
   honest gap: nothing has been trained.** The predecessor trained end
   to end with recorded runs; this repo has produced traces and proved
   they match. Both real; not the same achievement.
5. **Adopt or not? ADOPT PROVISIONALLY** — unhedged. The architecture
   passed every test it was given and no §9 condition ever fired;
   the outcome (production numerics, a training loop) is unproven.
   Converts to full ADOPT when CP-04′/CP-09′ passes with the replay run
   as written AND M4 runs one OPD loop (A-6 resolved). Reverses if
   CP-09′ shows a Polar-attributable mask/retokenization divergence with
   no fix in our code, if the slime bridge proves unusable at
   Path-C-rivaling cost, or if a re-vendor breaks the patch posture
   (A-8). While provisional: the predecessor stays frozen-alive, no
   production collection on the Mac pair.
