#### The findings, each with its owner

- **F1 — the engine sampling-defaults hazard (ours: estate config; fixed
  this CP).** The served mlx conversion ships no `generation_config.json`;
  an unpinned engine samples pi's parameterless requests at T=1.0, and
  NOTHING downstream can see it (no sampling on the trace, no wire
  persistence, gates green) — a clean-presenting class now recorded in
  `docs/checks-spec.md`. CP-07's episode was collected under it
  (structural claims unaffected). Cost going forward: every Polar estate
  must pin generation config explicitly and keep the request log on;
  trace-side checks cannot own this.
- **F2 — turn≥2 `response_logprobs` are conditioned on the wire context,
  not the merged stream (ours: ADR-0007 stitch semantics).** Verified
  causally twice over: replaying collected turn-2 against the engine's
  actual wire context (`prompt_ids[:-4] + response_ids[:3593]`) collapses
  the excess drift 0.0676 → 0.0133, matching the golden's own turn-2
  level (0.0159); and the adversarial workflow independently re-rendered
  the turn-2 wire prompt from the trace's own messages via the pinned
  template (calibrated by exactly reproducing turn-1's 2965 `prompt_ids`)
  and matched that reconstruction EXACTLY, 6554/6554 tokens. The token-in
  golden has no analogue — its wire context IS the stream. Two caveats
  the verification surfaced: (a) the de-stitch identity is
  **session-specific, not structural** — the merged stream keeps the RAW
  sampled ids for prior turns while the wire prompt carries the canonical
  re-render; they coincided here (verified: the re-render of turn-1's
  166-token body retokenizes identically), but the vendored builder's own
  header warns responses "can re-tokenize in the next prompt", and a
  session where they diverge breaks the de-stitch recipe even though the
  merge succeeds; (b) the raw turn-2 wire body is not persisted anywhere
  at the pin (row 7), so wire-exactness is established by calibrated
  re-render + the stitch invariants + logprob corroboration, not by
  diffing a logged engine prompt. Cost: trainers consuming
  `response_logprobs` as behavior-policy values inherit a
  4-token-per-prior-turn context approximation; any replay check must
  de-stitch via the config-pinned `generation_prompt_glue_ids`. Masks and
  token ids are untouched by this — the sampled ids remain engine-sampled
  verbatim.
- **F3 — vllm-metal cannot teacher-force (platform).**
  `prompt_logprobs_dict={}` is hardcoded (`vllm_metal/v1/model_runner.py:2235`;
  the decode-pipeline gate knows the flag but nothing computes them) and
  the `/v1/completions` `echo` path 500s (`KeyError` in
  `_create_completion_logprobs`). The contract's replay-through-the-engine
  is unexecutable on Mac estates; executed beside the engine instead. Per
  A-16, CP-09′ on the H200 runs the replay as written and its verdict
  governs the numerics; this Mac verdict fully screens structure.
- **F4 — the replay tolerance anchor does not transfer (platform).** The
  0.005/0.05 bounds anchor to the predecessor's same-engine CP-18
  measurement; the beside-the-engine floor measured here is mean
  0.007–0.016 on BOTH stacks symmetrically. The capture-vs-capture
  comparison on identical contexts (mean 0.000114) needs no re-anchoring
  and is the instrument that actually validates capture on this platform.
- **F5 — the row-27 exact-`0.0` artifact recurs Polar-side (platform).**
  15/363 vs the golden's 20/292; measured to be bf16 rounding of
  genuinely-near-zero RAW logprobs (the renormalization theory fits the
  data ~6× worse). CP-10 platform-conditions the suspicious-zero rule
  with the same evidence on both stacks.
