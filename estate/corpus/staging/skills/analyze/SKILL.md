# Skill: analyze

Produce an analysis of the case with a findings table.

## Instructions

1. Work through the case pages in `md/`, using `mcp_gsj_search_case` before
   grep for content questions.
2. Produce a findings table with the columns
   `| # | finding | supporting pages | fact or inference |`, citing
   supporting pages as `page:N`.
3. Below the table, discuss the two most consequential findings; every
   claim in the discussion cites `page:N`, and inferences are marked per
   AGENTS.md.
4. Write the analysis to `out/<task_id>.md`.
